QUESTION: Who has been elected as the President of the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA), serving a one-year term from September 2026?
A. Annalena Baerbock
B. Mirza Fakhrul Alamgir
C. Khalilur Rahman
D. Peter Pellegrini
E. Hakainde Hichilema
ANSWER: C
SOLUTION: Bangladesh's Khalilur Rahman is the President of the 81st UNGA, succeeding Germany's Annalena Baerbock, who presided over the 80th session.
