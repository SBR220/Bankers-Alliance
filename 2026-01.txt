QUESTION: National Youth Day in India is observed every year on which date?
A. 2 October
B. 12 January
C. 15 August
D. 5 September
ANSWER: B
SOLUTION: National Youth Day is observed on <b>12 January</b>, the birth anniversary of Swami Vivekananda (born 1863).

QUESTION: How many members does the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of the Reserve Bank of India have?
A. 4
B. 5
C. 6
D. 7
ANSWER: C
SOLUTION: The MPC has <b>six members</b>: three from the RBI (including the Governor, who chairs it) and three external members appointed by the Government of India.
TAG: Monetary Policy Committee, RBI, Banking news/Oneliner

QUESTION: The Ramsar Convention, signed in 1971, is an international treaty for the conservation and wise use of which of the following?
A. Tiger reserves
B. Coral reefs
C. Wetlands
D. Mangrove forests
ANSWER: C
SOLUTION: The <b>Ramsar Convention</b> (Ramsar, Iran, 1971) protects <mark>wetlands of international importance</mark>. World Wetlands Day is observed on 2 February.
TAG: Ramsar sites, Environment/Oneliner

TEXT: <b>Consider the following statements about the Unified Payments Interface (UPI):</b>
&#8203;
I. UPI was developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI).
II. UPI allows instant bank-to-bank transfers using a Virtual Payment Address.
III. UPI is regulated by SEBI.
&#8203;
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
QUESTION: STATEMENT BASED QUESTION
A. Only I
B. Only I and II
C. Only II and III
D. I, II and III
ANSWER: B
SOLUTION: UPI was developed by <b>NPCI</b> and enables instant bank-to-bank transfers via a VPA. Statement III is wrong: payment systems are regulated by the <b>RBI</b>, not SEBI.

TEXT: <b>The table below gives hypothetical UPI transaction data (in crore) for three months:</b>
<table><tr><th>Month</th><th>Transactions</th></tr><tr><td>January</td><td>1,200</td></tr><tr><td>February</td><td>1,500</td></tr><tr><td>March</td><td>1,800</td></tr></table>
&#8203;
What is the percentage increase in transactions from January to March?
QUESTION: DATA BASED QUESTION
A. 25%
B. 40%
C. 50%
D. 60%
ANSWER: C
SOLUTION: Increase = 1,800 - 1,200 = 600. Percentage increase = 600 / 1,200 x 100 = <b>50%</b>.
TAG: UPI, Banking news/Data Based
