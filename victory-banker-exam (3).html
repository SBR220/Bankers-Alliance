Instructions

Duration & Auto-Submission

  • The total duration of the Descriptive Test is 30 minutes.
  • The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen will display the remaining time, and will continue running throughout the test.
  • Once the timer reaches zero, the test will auto-submit. You do not need to manually submit your answers.

Test Structure

The Descriptive Writing Test consists of 3 questions:

  • The total marks for the Descriptive Test are 30 marks.
  • Marks will be allotted based on the evaluation criteria mentioned below.

Navigation Rules

  • Do not click the "Submit" button until you have attempted all questions, as the test cannot be resumed once submitted.
  • The word count for each answer is displayed at the bottom-right corner of the answer box.
  • You may switch between sections at any time before the timer ends using the section tabs.

Scoring Criteria

  • Your score will be calculated based on the following three criteria:
  • Format – Adherence to the required structure (Email, Report, Situation Analysis, and Precis).
  • Content – Relevance, clarity, and completeness of the response.
  • Language – Grammar, coherence, fluency, vocabulary, and sentence structure.
  • Marks will be deducted for exceeding or falling short of the word limit.
  • Email Writing will be evaluated on professionalism, tone, and clarity.
  • Report Writing will be assessed on organization, factual accuracy, and logical flow.
  • Situation Analysis will be judged on problem identification, analysis, and the solution suggested.
  • Precis Writing will be checked for conciseness, accuracy, and retention of key points from the passage.

Post-Test Information

  • After submitting the test, you will be able to view your score and a section-wise analysis.
